Full drawing toolkit完整的繪圖工具

Pencil, brush, eraser, shapes, fill, eyedropper, text.鉛筆、毛筆、橡皮擦、圖形、填色、取色器、文字。

Unlimited layers圖層系統

Add, duplicate, merge, reorder, and control opacity per layer — up to 50 per canvas.新增、複製、合併、排序、個別控制透明度——每個畫布最多 50 層。

Live collaboration即時協作

Draw together in real time. Every participant gets their own layer, lockable to prevent accidental edits.多人即時共畫，每個人有自己的圖層，可以鎖定避免被誤改。

IPFS backupIPFS 備份

Back up and restore your canvas to IPFS via Pinata or your own node — content-addressed, no vendor lock-in.透過 Pinata 或自架節點備份、還原到 IPFS，內容定址、不綁定單一服務商。

Installable & offline可安裝、離線使用

A real PWA — install it like a native app and keep drawing with no connection.真正的 PWA，可以像原生 App 一樣安裝，沒網路也能繼續畫。

Works everywhere跨裝置支援

Full touch support on mobile and tablets, auto-saves to your device, and picks a portrait or landscape canvas to match your screen.手機、平板完整支援觸控，自動存到裝置本機，並依螢幕自動選擇直式或橫式畫布。
